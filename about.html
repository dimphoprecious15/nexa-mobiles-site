About Nexa Mobiles

Nexa Mobiles started as a small side business selling Apple iPhones to friends, family, and referrals across South Africa. What began informally over WhatsApp has grown into a trusted source for quality iPhones — built entirely on word of mouth and repeat customers.

We believe buying a phone shouldn't feel like a gamble. Every device we sell is checked for authenticity and condition before it reaches you, and you can always reach a real person if something's wrong.

Our Mission

To make genuine, reliable Apple iPhones accessible and affordable to South African customers through honest pricing and personal service.

Our Vision

To grow into a trusted, well-known local retailer recognised for quality iPhones and dependable customer care.

Why Choose Us

  • Genuine Apple devices only
  • Direct, personal communication — no call centres
  • Competitive pricing on new and pre-owned models
